# Game event profiles

Use footage, game audio, and match context to locate candidate moments. Treat each cue as evidence, not a guarantee; capture the observed time range and confidence in the candidate record. Do not depend on fixed UI coordinates, resolutions, HUD layouts, or language-specific labels.

## Valorant

Look for eliminations and rapid multikills, aces, clutch wins, decisive entry picks, spike plants or defuses under pressure, ability combinations that create a turning point, and round-end reactions. Corroborate visual changes with kill-feed movement, announcer or game-audio cues, round state, and the surrounding action.

Favor moments with a clear setup, payoff, and enough lead-in for viewers to understand why the play matters. Avoid selecting a kill solely because a single HUD element changed.

## PUBG

Look for knocks and eliminations, multi-knock sequences, revives under threat, late-circle rotations, survival in shrinking zones, vehicle escapes or engagements, long-range shots, squad wipes, and chicken-dinner-ending plays. Corroborate with combat audio, player movement, zone pressure, inventory or map context when visible, and the aftermath.

Favor events whose stakes are visible from the play and its consequence. Avoid treating a map marker, kill feed, or fixed screen region as sufficient evidence by itself.
